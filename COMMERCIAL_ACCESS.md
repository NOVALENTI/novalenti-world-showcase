# Commercial evaluation and licensing

**NOVALENTI WORLD is a privately developed project by Valentino Herrera.** This public repository is a case study, not a source-code release or a commercial-use license.

## How interested companies can work with this project

1. **Introductory discussion:** public case study and static preview only. No private source, internal access, credentials, or unreleased media.
2. **Confidential evaluation:** a private demonstration may be arranged after a mutually accepted written confidentiality/evaluation agreement. Access is limited in time and purpose; no source delivery is implied.
3. **Paid pilot or commissioned development:** before work begins, both parties sign a written agreement identifying exact deliverables, milestone dates, acceptance criteria, expense responsibility, and payment terms. An initial payment or funded milestone is required before committing resources.
4. **Commercial software license:** source access, deployment rights, derivative works, exclusivity, sublicensing, and ownership transfer are **not included by default**. Any granted rights, fees, royalties, duration, geographic territory, and reporting obligations must be written into a separately signed license.
5. **Access delivery:** only the specifically licensed materials are handed over, after the agreed initial payment and any other contractual conditions are met. Credentials and unrelated proprietary systems are never part of the delivery.
